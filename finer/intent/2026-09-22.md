## What it decides

- **Compute:** no worker VMs. Every build, test and measurement runs on this host; plan text that
  depends on the pool is removed, not deferred.
- **Losses:** no library may end as a recorded loss. "Almost tie" is the floor, a win the target.
  A loss is diagnosed on both sides: the port's `.lil` (use types, purity and the language's other
  capabilities so the compiler has room) and the compiler (finer heuristics).
- **The plan serves the objective:** comply with the migration plan, but update it where the
  objective or the new architecture's evidence says it is wrong or stale.
- **The architecture's promise is on trial:** the rewrite was expected to buy faster compilation
  and better optimization from better internal typing. Investigate whether it does, and why not
  where it does not.
