Context in which it arrived: markedlil wins the 660-case spec loop (+8.3%) and loses the 32x single
document (-6.2%, reproduced in Node and Chromium on min-of-N). The proposal on the table had been to
flip `javascript.priority` from `size-first` to a performance policy for the whole port. This brief
refuses that: the objective stays size-first, and the compiler grows the ability to hold a different
behaviour in a named part of the program.
