# Compiler measurement tools and historical experiments

The [migration plan](../docs/migration/plan.md) owns the active design and work order; [its checklist](../docs/migration/index.md) owns progress, and [current status](../docs/current-status.md) reports evidence. Previous plans and snapshots are in [old-history](../docs/old-history/README.md). Do not resume retired work from an old task list or hypothesis note.

This folder retains useful [measurement tools](tools/), [source fixtures](tools/fixtures/), [competitor research](refs/competitor-techniques.md), historical [hypotheses](hypotheses/), [results](log.md), a historical [status snapshot](status.md), and original [owner briefs](intent/). These are evidence and tools, not a second architecture or migration coordinator.

The retained fleet inventory is [maintained-workloads.json](../benchmarks/libraries/maintained-workloads.json). Its required Marked and Probe test inventories are stored beside it. Relocation preserves every workload and required test; historical dispositions are not new acceptance decisions.

`tools/migration-progress.mjs` validates the archived 001–014 milestone table and its historical receipt ledger. It does not interpret or certify the active checklist.
