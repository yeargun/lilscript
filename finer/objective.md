# LilScript objective

The [active migration plan](../docs/migration/plan.md#1-objective-and-boundaries) owns the objective: smaller correct delivered programs under each selected raw/gzip/Brotli objective, with declared runtime requirements and configurable compilation effort.

Its [configuration contract](../docs/migration/plan.md#3-configuration-is-a-product-contract) separates semantics, codec settings, effort, runtime permissions and optional transformations. Exact candidate scores do not imply a global optimum; additional effort explores more while retaining the best valid result.

[Progress](../docs/migration/index.md) and [current evidence](../docs/current-status.md) live in their own pages. Original [owner briefs](intent/) remain evidence.
