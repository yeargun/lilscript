are implemented, with the final batch evidence recorded below. Q2 follows.
The [public ABI scope decision](s4-public-abi.md) replaces the previous
unspecified wider-transport gate without claiming those proposals implemented.

[Final evidence](../../benchmarks/migration-results/2026-10-01-s4-boundary-admission/README.md):
49 distinct focused checks, 18 artifact oracles, byte-identical supported
controls and the retained snapshot/reentry counterexample. Earlier S4 batches
and their JavaScript/native evidence remain in history. The explicit ABI scope
correction is part of completion, not an assertion of universal transport.

Predicted effect: checked identities and one fact owner remove ambiguous
transport and redundant work; precise contracts expose safe folding and smaller
representations. New capability/definite-assignment diagnostics must retain
useful spans and the documented migration contract. Size and compilation work
are measured separately under raw/gzip/Brotli; correctness takes precedence over
an apparent compression win.
