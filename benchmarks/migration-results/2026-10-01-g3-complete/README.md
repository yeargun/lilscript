logical assignments, guarded receiver aliases, and legal declaration locality.

and Brotli-11. Four development workloads cover spellings, receivers, modern

forms being compared and apply equally to both versions. Six previous G2
open/closed cases reuse G2's pinned evidence and check G3 against the same
independent numeric/public-shape oracle. These are affected checks, not a fleet,
held-out, native or competitor qualification.
