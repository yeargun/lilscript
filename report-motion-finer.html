Drift root-cause findings

The first complete run failed rather than being rounded into a pass. Inspection separated harness defects from missing Motion control paths and cold-start allocation overhead.

FindingEffect measuredStatus
Correctness waited for Playwright networkidle, adding 500 ms after module evaluation and sampling the 350 ms stagger after it had ended. Both npm and LilScript falsely failed the mid-animation cascade check. fixed — animation-start fixtures now continue at document load
The hybrid animate() path treated an explicit CSS transform as a JS MotionValue, while the mini controller lacked timeline playback controls. One hybrid target did not animate and two scroll-linked WAAPI lanes stayed at zero. fixed — native transforms route through WAAPI and mini controls expose timeline time
Every inner mini animation eagerly allocated completion Promises, callback closures, keyframe copies, and dynamic method wrappers even when the returned group was ignored. Cold scheduling initially missed the non-inferiority confidence bound. fixed — completion is lazy, active-map cleanup is direct, and internals use typed dispatch
CDP heap was sampled without forcing collection. Warm results depended on V8's incidental GC timing rather than retained library state. fixed — every retained-heap sample follows HeapProfiler.collectGarbage

Final 192-element scheduling ratios are 0.972× warm and 0.907× cold. Forced-GC retained-heap ratios are 0.976× warm and 0.954× cold. All paired-bootstrap upper bounds remain within the 1.15× non-inferiority budget.

Methodology

  • Size lane: Vite 8.2.1, minify on, Node v22.21.1, LilScript mode closed-world.
  • Correctness: ten paired animation, CSS-variable, stagger, spring, scroll, gesture, viewport, resize, and MotionValue fixtures.
  • Perf: 192 elements × two animated properties, randomized lil/npm order, 8 warmup discarded, n=201, mean/median/p95, paired-bootstrap 95% upper ratios via benchmarks/statistics.mjs.
  • Cold vs warm measured separately; retained heap is Chromium CDP JSHeapUsedSize after explicit collection.
